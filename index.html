Frequently Asked Questions

Is my financial data safe with Flowcent?

Absolutely. We use end-to-end encryption and bank-level security protocols to protect your data. Your privacy and security are our top priorities.

How does the AI work?

Our AI is trained to understand natural language for financial transactions. You can say "I spent $20 on coffee" or "Split a $50 dinner with John," and it will categorize and log it for you.

Can I use Flowcent on multiple devices?

Yes, your Flowcent account syncs seamlessly across all your devices, so you always have your financial information at your fingertips.

The security of your data is paramount. We are committed to protecting your information and have implemented robust security measures.

Encryption: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS).
